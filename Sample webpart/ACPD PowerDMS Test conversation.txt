As a result, FCPD may request ACPD assistance for incidents requiring specialized tactical capabilities. ​1​​​2​

The agreement states that ERT-type incidents include situations such as:

Barricaded suspects
Hostage situations
Active shooters
Other violent incidents requiring a rapid armed response ​1​​​2​
The MOA further provides that:

The response force should include, at a minimum, a command-and-control element, a hostage negotiation team, and an entry team. ​1​​​2​
When FCPD needs this assistance, it will request help directly through Arlington County dispatch/Emergency Communications. ​1​​​2​
ACPD may support, assist, and render aid to FCPD officers when requested by the Falls Church Chief of Police, a designated representative, or the FCPD watch commander, particularly when Falls Church lacks sufficient staffing, equipment, or certified personnel. ​1​​​2​
If the Arlington County ERT is activated, the ACPD ERT Commander and the FCPD Incident Commander will operate under a Unified Command structure. ​1​​​2​
FCPD remains responsible for support functions such as evacuating nearby buildings, coordinating utility shutdowns, and establishing an outer perimeter. ​1​​​2​
In a rapidly evolving active-shooter event where there is an immediate threat to life, ACPD officers are authorized to coordinate directly with FCPD personnel, develop an entry plan, and enter the location to stop the threat without waiting for a formal command structure to be established. ​1​​​2​
The agreement was executed by Falls Church Police Chief Harry Reitze and Arlington County Police Chief M. Douglas Scott in 2008. ​2​
